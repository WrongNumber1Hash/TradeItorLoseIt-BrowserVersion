📖 Forex Trading Game Rules & Mechanics

1. Core Setup & 3-Pile Allocation

Each player starts with $100 Cash. Before each round, divide your cash into 3 piles:

  • Bet (Principal): Your active exposure multiplied by 25x leverage ($20 bet = $500 total exposure).
  • Collateral (Buffer): Reserved margin to absorb negative pip swings.
  • Uninvested Reserve: Safe liquid cash protected from market moves in the current round.

2. Card Mechanics & Pip Movement

  • Money Cards (2-10): Sets base move (1 point = 100 pips) based on Risk-On sentiment vs. Safe-Haven / Risk-Off demand. 🔴 Red = USD Strength | ⬛ Black = JPY Strength.
  • Event Cards (J, Q, K, A): Adds volatility modifier (+1 to +4 points) mapped to real economic calendar events (FOMC, NFP, CPI, BoJ Rate releases, MoF intervention).
  • Catalyst Days: High volatility scheduled rounds that automatically add an extra +1 Point (+100 pips) boost.

3. Settlement, Rollovers & Liquidation

  • Winners: Collect profit proportional to position size and pip movement.
  • Losers (Loss ≤ Collateral): Loss deducted from buffer. Choose to close position OR pay a $5 Carrying Fee to roll over into the next round.
  • Liquidation (Loss > Collateral): Position wiped out! Entire Bet + Collateral forfeited to the bank.

📚 Simulation Glossary & Terms

A. Core Capital & Position Piles

  • Total Cash Bankroll: Your overall liquid account balance starting at $100.
  • Bet (Principal): The portion of cash risked as the base position for the round.
  • Margin Exposure: Your Bet multiplied by 25x leverage ($20 bet = $500 margin).
  • Collateral (Buffer): Cash set aside to absorb negative pip swings and losses.
  • Uninvested Reserve: Safe liquid cash protected from market moves in the current round.

B. Market Mechanics & Cards

  • Money Cards (2–10): Reflect Risk-On appetite vs. Risk-Off / Safe-Haven flows. 🔴 Red = USD strength | ⬛ Black = JPY strength.
  • Event Cards (J, Q, K, A): Volatility modifiers (+1 to +4 points) tied to macro trading calendar releases.
  • Catalyst Days: High-volatility scheduled rounds that automatically add an extra +1 Point (+100 pips) boost.

C. Outcomes & Risk Management

  • Long USD / Short JPY: Betting that USD strengthens against JPY (USD/JPY ↗).
  • Short USD / Long JPY: Betting that JPY strengthens against USD (USD/JPY ↙).
  • Rollover ($5 Fee): Paying a $5 fee from reserve to carry a losing trade into the next day.
  • Liquidation: Triggered when a trade loss exceeds your Collateral buffer ($Loss > Collateral$), forfeiting Bet + Collateral.
